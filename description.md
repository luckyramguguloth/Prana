The Series
Four battlegrounds. One national title.

iQOO City Battles 2026 is a connected four-city series across Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai, and Hyderabad. The bridge
Office Kit.

Office Kit links the iQOO phone to your laptop: screen, clipboard, files, and input, across one connection. During Red Light the laptop is closed as a build machine, so Office Kit is the only route between the two: you mirror the laptop, drive it from the keyboard, and move files without a cable. During Green Light it's how the phone stays in the loop while the laptop does the heavy compute.

It is also scored. Office Kit usage is 10% of the rubric, read off HackTracker device data rather than self-reporting: counts and durations only. Pairing is covered in the Saturday teach-in, and your loaner device arrives with it already set up.
Download for laptop

Official download · pc.vivoglobal.com

    Laptop: Windows 10 or later · macOS 10.14.6 or later
    Phone: iQOO phone on OriginOS 6, pre-installed on the loaner you collect at check-in

Screen mirror

The phone UI on the laptop display, live. Demo, debug, and drive the build without picking the device up.
Shared clipboard

Copy on one device, paste on the other. Tokens, snippets, prompts, and logs move without a chat window in between.
File transfer

Drag and drop files or whole folders between phone and laptop. No size limit, no format restriction, no cable.
Remote control

Laptop keyboard and trackpad driving the phone. Type into the device at full speed during Red Light.

Rules & guidelines.

Please read before you build.
Phone-first format

    The iQOO device is the build surface and the demo surface: every entry must run and pitch on the phone.
    A local or open-source model at the core earns brownie points, with the phone in the loop via Office Kit.
    Office Kit bridges phone and laptop (screen mirror, clipboard, file transfer, remote control). Pairing instructions ship with the loaner and are covered in the Saturday 10:00 teach-in. On-device inference targets the Snapdragon NPU. AI credits for the weekend are free.
    Stacks welcome: native Android, Flutter, React Native, or PWA. If it runs on the phone with a local or open-source model at the core, it qualifies.

30-hour city battle

    Each City Battle is a 30-hour all-inclusive event: Sat around 08:00 check-in through Sun around 17:00 awards.
    Green Light = both devices; Red Light = iQOO phone only via Office Kit. Two evaluation rounds (Sat evening, Sun morning) feed a Top 10 pitch.
    Top 6 teams per city advance to the Grand Finale in Bengaluru on Oct 9–11, 2026: 3 student teams and 3 working-professional teams. The Finale itself runs 48 hours, Friday evening to Sunday evening. Standout teams beyond the Top 6 can also earn Finale slots, and you can register directly for the Grand Finale without going through a city battle.

Your iQOO device

    One flagship iQOO loaner phone per person, handed over at Saturday check-in with HackTracker pre-installed and Office Kit already paired.
    Devices remain iQOO property. Keep them in the venue / designated hacking zone and return them before exit.
    HackTracker captures counts and durations only (no keystrokes, screenshots, or browsing) for creative phone use and Office Kit scoring.

Tracks & buckets

    Seven tracks in the city battles; the Grand Finale runs six, dropping FinTech and Commerce, Smart Education and HealthTech and adding Mobility and Community App.
    Tracks are open to students and working professionals, but teams cannot mix buckets.
    Tracks are broad domains, not fixed briefs. Anything that fits is fair game. Open Innovation is the wildcard everywhere.

Build rules

    Original work only: code written during the event window. No shipping a pre-built product.
    Open-source libraries and frameworks are fine with attribution; carrying in a completed app is not.
    Submit your repo and demo assets on the Reskilll platform before the hard cutoff (repos locked before Top 10 pitches). Late submissions may incur scoring penalties or disqualification.
    Cheating, plagiarism, or unfair practice means immediate disqualification. Organisers may verify a project was built inside the event window.

Judging

    Two scored evaluation rounds plus a Top 10 final pitch, demo on the iQOO phone. HackTracker supplies phone-use and Office Kit scores; jury scores product quality, novelty, depth, and pitch.
    Weights: End product 30% · Novelty 20% · Creative phone use 15% · Technical depth 15% · Office Kit 10% · Demo 10%.
    Top 6 teams per city advance (three per bucket); standouts beyond Top 6 can also earn Finale slots. Hit a technical issue before your slot? Tell an organiser immediately.

Code of conduct

    Respect everyone: participants, mentors, jury, organisers, volunteers. Every background and skill level is welcome.
    Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disruptive behaviour: immediate removal.
    Help others debug when you can. If something feels wrong, speak to any organiser immediately.

Venue rules

    Report on time: check-in closes per the city schedule.
    No smoking on premises. No alcohol on-site.
    Stay in designated zones (hacking floor, rest area, meal zone). Keep noise considerate of neighbouring teams.

Safety & wellbeing

    First-aid support is on site. In an emergency, alert the nearest organiser immediately.
    Take breaks: a team that rests ships better code, especially through the night sprint.
    Tell a teammate or volunteer if you step out after dark.

Reaching organisers

    On the floor: grab any organiser or volunteer at any time.
    A direct contact is shared at check-in. The query desk stays open through close.
    Series contact: sameera@reskilll.com

Photography & media

    Event filming and photography support iQOO and Reskilll documentation and promotion.
    Consent at check-in covers event media use. Do not film or share another team’s screen or build without permission.

Standard Terms

Eligibility, intellectual property, loaner-hardware responsibility, prizes and tax, liability, and how these rules can change are all covered in one place.
